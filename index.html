Digital Healthcare Infrastructure
Healthcare - Data Engineering - Cameroon - 2025
MbaoMed

A digital patient registration prototype targeting two critical failures in Cameroonian hospitals: catastrophic data loss from handwritten records, and unmanageable queue times that cost patients hours and clinics their credibility.

Born from direct empirical observation of the contrast between U.S. and Cameroonian healthcare data systems during my exchange year in Iowa, where digitized records transformed care delivery. Coming home, the gap was impossible to ignore. I conceptualized and led every aspect of this project, from problem framing and architecture to workflow design and testing, working resourcefully with the tools available to me. A research paper on this work is in preparation for publication.

This is not a school exercise. It is the first step of a career built around one observation: Cameroon's clinics fail patients because of data problems, not just funding problems.
