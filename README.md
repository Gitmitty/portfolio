## About

This repository contains the source code for my personal portfolio, designed to showcase my academic journey, research interests, leadership experiences, international exchange programs, AI-assisted software projects, and commitment to using technology for social impact.

Rather than serving as a traditional résumé, this portfolio tells the story of a student who believes artificial intelligence should be used to solve real problems facing underserved communities.

My long-term goal is to contribute to research at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, healthcare, education, and sustainable development while continuing to build solutions that create measurable social impact.

### Projects

#### 🏥 MediFast Cameroon

An AI-assisted healthcare platform exploring how digital technologies can improve access to healthcare services in Cameroon through appointment scheduling, hospital discovery, emergency resources, and patient-centered design.

#### 🌿 Mboamed

A community-focused initiative inspired by observations of local environmental challenges and strengthened by lessons learned during my Kennedy-Lugar YES exchange year in the United States.

The project explores how technology, community participation, and artificial intelligence can work together to support environmental sustainability and local development.
